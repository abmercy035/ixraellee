Philosophy is not confined to lecture halls or old textbooks. It lives in how we make decisions, evaluate evidence, and build relationships.

## Key Reflections

- **Observation before judgment**: Taking time to understand context.
- **Intellectual humility**: Being willing to change one's view when presented with new facts.
- **Purposeful action**: Aligning daily routines with long-term principles.
